What I do

Translation

Literary, tourism and business texts, produced with CAT tools (Trados, Phrase) and termbases for consistent terminology.

Subtitling

Translation and spotting (timing) of subtitles for films, news reports and talks, following Netflix and TED guidelines, using Aegisub and CaptionHub.

Proofreading and post-editing

Review of machine-translated texts in two stages: a bilingual check against the original, then a final English-only polish.

Specialisms

  • Literary translation
  • Tourism and hospitality
  • Film and video subtitling
  • Business and institutional texts
  • Machine translation post-editing

About me

I am a Manchester-based translator working primarily from French into English.

Having studied French, German and Italian to an undergraduate level, I have lived and studied in Austria and Italy and completed a hospitality work placement in France.

Most recently, I graduated with an MA in Translation and Interpreting at the University of Manchester, covering both translation for the creative industries and for businesses and institutions.
