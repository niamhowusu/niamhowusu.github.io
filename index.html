Services

Translation, subtitling and proofreading from French and Italian into English.

Translation

Literary, tourism and business texts, produced with CAT tools (Trados, memoQ, Phrase) and termbases for consistent terminology.

Subtitling

Translation and spotting (timing) of subtitles for films, news reports and talks, following Netflix and TED guidelines, using Aegisub and CaptionHub.

Proofreading and post-editing

Review of machine-translated texts in two stages: a bilingual check against the original, then a final English-only polish.

Specialisms

  • Literary translation
  • Tourism and hospitality
  • Film and video subtitling
  • Business and institutional texts
  • Machine translation post-editing

About me

I’m a Manchester-based translator working from French and Italian into English. I recently completed an MA in Translation and Interpreting at the University of Manchester, covering both creative and business translation, with a dissertation in literary translation. I’ve lived and studied in Salzburg and Padua, and completed a hospitality work placement in Brive, France, which feeds directly into my tourism work.
